Company

Built in Accra. Trusted everywhere.

Verid was built for the realities of fast-moving digital economies: businesses that need to verify identity in seconds, without treating customers like a compliance problem to be processed.

Our mission

Identity should move with the customer, not against them.

We help digital businesses verify people with speed, clarity, and respect. The result is safer onboarding, better customer experiences, and decisions teams can stand behind.

“The best verification experience is the one customers never have to struggle with.”

Why we exist

We built Verid to make identity verification feel less like friction and more like trust.

From banks to telecoms to digital service platforms, the teams we work with need identity checks that are secure, easy to understand, and respectful of the people behind the documents.

Values

How we work.

Human dignity

Every interaction should respect the person being verified, not just the business behind the flow.

Operational clarity

Teams should understand what happened and why, without translating raw signals into decisions by hand.

Design for trust

Verification experiences should feel calm and efficient, not intimidating, rigid, or confusing.
